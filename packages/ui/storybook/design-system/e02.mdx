# E02 — Book and pay

A signed-in mentee sees the price, picks a slot, writes the question and pays on the simulated gateway, and can cancel under the R14 rule. Backlog: E02-S01, E02-S02, E02-S01-T01. Spec: book-and-pay (design, not implemented). Sources: R03, R11, R14, R17, R21, R22, D13, D18.

## Flow

1. On the mentor page, a bookable slot is a “Book” link.
2. New booking: length with its price, the question, and the cancellation rule; a slot already taken or an unpaid booking is explained instead of the form.
3. Payment: the simulated notice, the hold deadline once, the price change notice, pay, edit, abandon.
4. Details: the booking, the status, money facts, cancellation with the expected outcome, refunds in progress.
5. My bookings: the list, unpaid first.
