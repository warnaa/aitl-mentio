# E05 — Mentor earnings

A mentor sees what they earned per session: the price minus Mentio's commission. No money moves on the mock build. Backlog: E05-S01. **No E05 spec yet**: the screens show the book-and-pay `MentorEarning` rows and follow the user decisions of 2026-11-01. Sources: R06, R10, R14, R16, R21, D11–D14.

## Flow

1. A session that is held, a mentee no-show or a late cancellation records an earning (E02 Phase 3, E04-S02).
2. Earnings: totals as price − commission = share, then one row per session.
3. A row opens the session details with its own breakdown.
