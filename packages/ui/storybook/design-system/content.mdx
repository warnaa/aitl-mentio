# Content

The app speaks Polish (R15). Stories and these docs are in English by the task's instruction, and the app passes its Polish copy through props (F1). The rules apply in both languages.

## Tone

Second person, warm and collegial, the way you would write to a colleague. Warmth comes from words, not decoration. Plain and specific: name the mentor, the time and the amount. No emoji or exclamation marks added by the interface. Sources: U01.1; R02.5; character words (warm, focused).

## Buttons and links

- **Buttons:** a verb and an object — “Pay 240 zł”, “Publish page”, “Cancel booking”, “Accept question”. Never “OK”, “Submit” or “Yes”. Sources: task delete-confirmation rules; WCAG 2.4.6.
- **Confirmations:** the confirm button repeats the action, and the way out says what is kept: “Cancel booking” / “Keep booking”, “Decline and refund” / “Keep question”. Source: task delete-confirmation rules.
- **Pending labels:** describe the ongoing action — “Paying…”, “Sending…”. Source: platform F5.
- **Links:** name the destination — “Back to Alex’s page”. Never “Click here”. Source: WCAG 2.4.4.

## Errors

Say what happened, then what to do, close to the cause:
- “The session ended at 18:50, so this message was not sent. Your text is still in the field.”
- “Check your connection, then try again.” (the fixed network message)

Never show technical text, codes or stack traces. Auth errors stay generic (“invalid credentials”). Sources: task Content; platform F1; AGENTS.md security baseline.

Field errors state the fix, and differ from the placeholder: “Choose 25 or 50 minutes”. Source: lesson 2026-10-31.

## Empty states

Say why it is empty and the next step:
- “You have no bookings yet. When you book a text session with a mentor, it appears here.”
- “No paid questions yet … Share your page link to get the first booking.”

“No results for these filters” is a separate state, with “Clear filters”. Sources: task Content; task List with filters pattern.

## Length and translation

- Labels stay one line where possible.
- Status labels stay under about 40 characters (approved batch-2 proposal 3).
- Polish is often longer than English, so every component has a long-content story; text wraps, it is never cut. Sources: task stage 2; WCAG 1.4.10.
