# E04 — The text session

At the slot time, the mentee and the mentor hold a 25- or 50-minute text session. A session that did not happen is settled by fault the next time either side opens it. Backlog: E04-S01, E04-S02. **No E04 spec yet**: the room follows the brief, references R02.1–R02.6 and the user decisions of 2026-11-01. Sources: R01, R02, R09, R10, D07, D16, N01; approved Q3, Q5.

## Flow

1. Before the start, both see the question and the R10 rule; there is no field.
2. At the start, the mentor has 10 minutes to post the first reply.
3. During the session: messages with author and time, code blocks, replies quoting their source, and sending that keeps the text on failure.
4. At the end, the conversation becomes a read-only transcript visible to the two participants.
5. On the next visit, a missed session is settled: the mentor's no-show is refunded, and the mentee's no-show pays the mentor.
