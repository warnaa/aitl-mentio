# E03 — Mentor decides

The mentor reads a paid question before the session and accepts or declines it; a decline is refunded at once, and a question not accepted by the start counts as declined. Backlog: E03-S01. **No E03 spec yet**: the screens follow the book-and-pay contract with E03 and the user decisions of 2026-11-01 (routes, endpoints, labels). Sources: R09, R12, R14, D19.

## Flow

1. A paid question appears on “My sessions”, waiting for a decision first.
2. The mentor opens it: facts, their share after commission, the R12 rule, Accept and Decline above the question.
3. Accept, or decline after a confirmation that names the refund.
4. The mentee sees the outcome on their booking; an unaccepted booking is settled on the next visit.
