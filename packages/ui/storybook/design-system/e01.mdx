# E01 — Mentor page and discovery

A mentor publishes a page with public work, a rate and slots, and gets a share link. Mentees find mentors by link or on an opt-in list. This page includes the accounts prerequisite (E01-S01-T01). Backlog: E01-S01–S04, E01-S01-T01. Spec: mentor-pages and accounts (designs, not implemented). Sources: backlog; D02, D09, D22, D23, N02, R08.

## Flow

1. A visitor registers or signs in; a mentor registers with `?jako=mentor` or becomes a mentor (D22).
2. The mentor edits the page — address, name, description, public-work links — and sets one rate per 25 minutes (90–600 zł).
3. The mentor publishes and copies the share link; they can opt into the public list.
4. The mentor publishes slots; mentees see only bookable ones (2 hours to 14 days ahead).
5. A mentee opens the link or the list and reads public work, prices and slots.
