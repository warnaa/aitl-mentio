# E03 — screens and states (preliminary)

E03 has no screen specification (approved stage-0 Q4). These screens follow E03-S01, R09, R12, R14 and D19, and the book-and-pay spec's contract with E03:
- `accepted` and `declined`;
- a decline releases the slot and refunds through the booking's single refund;
- R12 is applied before accepting;
- `settle` is open to the mentor.

Each state is a story under **Product › E03 › Screens**. Responses are simulated through the real `apiCall` (MSW).

**Decided by the user on 2026-11-01** (POSTEP 9–11, 15): the routes `/mentor/sesje` and `/mentor/sesje/[id]`, the navigation items “My sessions” and “Earnings”, `POST /api/bookings/[id]/accept` and `/decline`, the 409 code `booking_not_decidable`, the mentor-facing status labels, and placing the decision buttons above the question.
