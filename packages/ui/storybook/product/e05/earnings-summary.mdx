# EarningsSummary

**Preliminary.** E05 has no screen specification. The money model comes from the book-and-pay spec's `MentorEarning`; points marked **Proposal** wait for your decision or the E05 spec.

## 1. Purpose

Show a mentor exactly how their earnings are calculated: the price the mentee paid, minus Mentio's commission, gives their share. Sources: E05-S01; R06.

## 2. When to use

- The earnings page (`/mentor/zarobki`, user decision 2026-11-01): totals over all settled sessions.
- A settled session's details on the mentor's side: held, mentee no-show, or late cancellation.

Sources: E05-S01; R10, R14; book-and-pay `MentorEarning.reason`.

## 3. When not to use

- For a list of sessions, use DataTable with price and share columns.
- For sessions that earn nothing (declined, not accepted in time, mentor no-show): show no summary. The status and the notice say why.
- For payouts: they do not exist until D14.

Sources: R09, R12; D13, D14.

## 4. Anatomy

- A Card with an h2 title and an optional context line.
- A description list of price, commission and share.
- The share as the result line: semibold, after a thin separator.
- An optional note: the D13 simulation line.

Sources: R06; R01.3 (thin separator); WCAG 1.3.1.

## 5. Variants

There are no visual variants. Optional parts: `context` and `note`. One component serves totals and a single session. Sources: package API.

## 6. States

The summary is static. The reason the mentor earns comes through `context`:
- “Session held”;
- “Mentee no-show — the session counts as held”;
- “Late cancellation — …”.

Loading, error and empty states belong to the page: LoadingMessage, ErrorMessage and EmptyState, never a summary showing zeros. Interactive states do not apply. Sources: R10, R14; task data states.

<Unstyled><Canvas of={Stories.States} /></Unstyled>

## 7. Behavior and accessibility

- The `dl` pairs every amount with its term.
- The minus sign is part of the amount text (“−48 zł”), so it is read aloud, not drawn.
- The result line is distinguished by weight and position, not colour.
- Amounts use tabular numbers.
- There are no interactive parts.

Sources: WCAG 1.3.1, 1.4.1.

## 8. Content

- **Amounts:** whole złoty with the currency after the number, and thousands separated.
- **Commission term:** names the rate stored with the booking: “Mentio’s commission (20%)”.
- **Totals:** the price term is “Paid by mentees”.
- **Note:** says no money is paid out on the simulated build.

Never state a payout date: payouts wait for D14 and R16.

Sources: R06, R21, D10, D13, D14, R16; user decision 2026-10-31 (amounts shown as zł).

## 9. Responsiveness

The lines stop at 65ch so terms and amounts stay close. At 320 px a long term wraps and its amount moves to the next line, right-aligned. Sources: approved Q3; WCAG 1.4.10.

## 11. Code

```tsx
<EarningsSummary title="Twoje zarobki" context={`${totals.count} rozliczonych sesji`}
  price={{ term: 'Zapłacili podopieczni', amount: formatMoney(totals.priceGrosze) }}
  commission={{ term: `Prowizja Mentio (${formatPercent(commissionBps)})`, amount: `−${formatMoney(totals.commissionGrosze)}` }}
  share={{ term: 'Twoja część', amount: formatMoney(totals.shareGrosze) }}
  note="Symulacja — pieniądze nie są jeszcze wypłacane." />
```

Sources: book-and-pay `MentorEarning` fields; Proposal (totals endpoint).
