# E05 — screens and states (preliminary)

E05 has no screen specification (approved stage-0 Q4). The earnings page follows E05-S01, R06 and R21. It displays the book-and-pay spec's `MentorEarning` rows (price, commission, share, reason), which E02 Phase 3 and E04-S02 write. No money moves (D13): the page says so, and promises no payout (D14, R16). Each state is a story under **Product › E05 › Screens**.

**Decided by the user on 2026-11-01** (POSTEP 9, 14, 15):
- the route `/mentor/zarobki`;
- totals over all time, with no period filter;
- four columns (Session, Outcome, Price, Your share), within the approved four-column mobile limit; the commission per row is on the session's details;
- the outcome labels.
