# Next-visit settlement (preliminary)

Nothing in Mentio runs on a timer. A session that didn't happen, or a booking nobody accepted, is settled when either person next opens it. The screen first checks, then states the outcome and what it means for the money. Sources: E04-S02; R09, R10, R12; D13, D16.

## Rules

1. While the check runs, say what is happening: “Checking what happened in this session…”. If it fails, say that nothing changed and offer “Try again”. Sources: B9 settle; F1.
2. State the outcome as a fact with its rule, from the reader's side:
   - **Mentor no-show:** the mentee reads “Alex did not post within 10 minutes of the start”, and the mentor reads “You did not post…”.
   - **Mentee no-show:** the mentor reads “Kasia did not open the session”.
   - **Not accepted in time (R12):** the mentee reads “Alex did not accept before the session started”.

   Sources: R10, R12.
3. Name the money consequence on the same screen: “Your refund of 240 zł has started”, “Nothing is earned for it”, or the EarningsSummary for a paid mentor. A refund is “started” until it succeeds. Sources: R09, R06; B9.
4. Never promise when it happens (“today”, “within an hour”): no timers run, and the refund follows the next visit. Sources: D16 (“the same day” is no longer guaranteed).
5. A mentee who missed a held session keeps the mentor's answer. Sources: R10; R02 (the transcript stays private).
6. Outcomes are shown in place, as a note: no toast and no colour-only status. Sources: R02.4; WCAG 1.4.1.

## Composition
