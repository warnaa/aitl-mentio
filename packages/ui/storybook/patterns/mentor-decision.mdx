# Mentor decision (preliminary)

A mentor accepts or declines a paid question before its session, knowing exactly what each choice does to the mentee's money and their own slot. E03 has no screen specification, so this pattern is a proposal built from the rules below. Sources: E03-S01; R09, R12; the book-and-pay contract with E03.

## Rules

1. Show what the mentor decides on before the buttons: time, length, price, their share after the commission, and the question in the code role. Sources: E03-S01; R06; R01.2.
2. State the consequence of not deciding, as text, with no countdown: “If you do not accept before the session starts, the booking is declined and Kasia gets a full refund.” Sources: R12; D13/D16 (no timers); U02.1.
3. Accept is the primary action. Decline is an outline action with a confirmation that names the booking and the refund: “Decline and refund” / “Keep question”. Sources: R01.5; task delete-confirmation rules; R09.
4. The decision is visible without scrolling, and the question follows it (user decision 2026-11-01: the task's detail rule outweighs reading order). Sources: task detail pattern.
5. If the booking changed meanwhile (the start passed, or the mentee cancelled), the server refuses, and the screen shows the new state and why. A decision is never applied to a changed booking. Sources: book-and-pay (accept refuses at/after the start; cancel/decline race).
6. After deciding, the status and the money facts update in place. A refund is “started” until it succeeds, and a failed refund offers “Try again” (settle). Sources: B9; settle for the mentor.
7. The mentee sees the outcome on their booking with the next step: another mentor after a decline, or another time after R12. Sources: E03-S01; R12.

## Composition

BookingSummary (facts and status), ActionButton (accept; decline with `confirm`), Message (the question, `preformatted`), Alert (outcomes), DataTable (the list). Sources: approved stage-0 inventory; derived decision 3d (no MentorDecision component).
