# Booking and payment recovery

Money and slot states must never look more final than they are. This pattern shows how a mentee is guided through each unfinished payment state. Sources: book-and-pay UI/UX, B9 and the settle-on-mount rules.

## Rules

1. The payment screen always says the payment is simulated, and shows the hold deadline once, as text: no live countdown. Sources: D13; book-and-pay accessibility.
2. A price change is stated above the amount and action, and survives refreshes: “before … now …”. Sources: 2026-10-30 decision.
3. A pending charge reads “Payment in progress…”. When no result arrives, offer “Try again”, which runs one settle attempt. There is no automatic retry loop. Sources: book-and-pay settle-on-mount.
4. Declined, expired and cutoff are different outcomes with different texts. Never describe an unresolved or refunded charge as declined. Sources: book-and-pay UI/UX.
5. A pending refund is “started” or “in progress” until it succeeds. A failed refund says so, without a contact. Sources: B9; decision 2026-10-29.
6. Abandoning while a charge is pending tells the person the refund rule before they confirm. Sources: book-and-pay UI/UX.
7. A stale edit or pay (`409 booking_changed`) refreshes, keeps the person's input and asks for a fresh confirmation. Sources: book-and-pay UI/UX.
8. Outcomes stay on the page, next to the action: no toast. Sources: reference R02.4.
