# Cancellation

Before a person cancels, the page tells them exactly what will happen to their money, following the R14 table. Sources: book-and-pay R14 cancellation table; brief R14.

## Rules

1. The confirmation names the booking and the expected outcome: “You will receive a full refund of 240 zł”, or “Less than 12 hours remain before the session — cancelling gives no refund, and the mentor is paid.” Sources: book-and-pay UI/UX.
2. The request sends that outcome (`expectedOutcome`). If the terms changed meanwhile (`409 cancel_outcome_changed`), the person sees the new terms and confirms again; nothing is cancelled silently. Sources: book-and-pay API contract.
3. At or after the start, cancellation is unavailable, and the reason is shown next to the disabled button. Sources: R14; task What to avoid (disabled without explanation).
4. The confirm button is “Cancel booking”, and the way out is “Keep booking”, never “OK” and “Cancel”. Sources: task delete-confirmation rules.
5. After cancelling, the status and money facts update in place, with the refund shown as in progress until it succeeds. Sources: B9.
6. The rule is stated before booking: “You can cancel free of charge until the mentor accepts, and after that up to 12 hours before the session.” Sources: book-and-pay UI/UX (R14 line under the form).
