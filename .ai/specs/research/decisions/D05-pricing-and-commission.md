# D05 — Mentors set their own 25- and 50-minute prices within Mentio's PLN bounds; Mentio keeps a configurable 20% of each paid session; payments through Stripe Checkout, payouts through Stripe Connect
- Date, owner: 2026-10-27, Damian (founder C), confirmed in the om-discover refresh; closes Q02, supersedes the proposal R04
- Context and the options weighed: all three mentors want control over their price (2026-08-22, 2026-09-03 ×2). The board proposed 15–20% per session; Topmate takes 10% via an expert's own link and 20% via its marketplace (checked 2026-09-04). Options offered: one cut, a split cut by channel, no cut yet, a fixed platform price; then whether the cut is taken from the price or added on top, and whether to set the bounds now. Damian's own answers, not agent recommendations.
- Decision and why: one configurable commission, 20% at launch, on every paid session whether booked through the mentor's link or the public list, taken from the mentor's price (the mentee pays the price shown; the mentor receives the rest, minus Stripe's fees per D11). Bounds at launch: 90–600 PLN for 25 minutes, 180–1,200 PLN for 50 minutes. Mentees pay with Stripe Checkout; mentors are paid through Stripe Connect. The rate and the bounds are business choices, not measured or tested figures.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: prices are in PLN only. Revisit if invited mentors object to the cut or the bounds, or if bookings through mentors' own links dominate.
- Status: active, except the R05 part (two separately set prices), superseded by D15 (2026-10-28): one rate per 25 minutes (R08)
