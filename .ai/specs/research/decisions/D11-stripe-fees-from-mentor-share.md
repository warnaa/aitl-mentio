# D11 — Stripe's processing and payout fees come out of the mentor's share, not out of Mentio's commission
- Date, owner: 2026-10-27, Damian (founder C), confirmed in the om-discover refresh; refines R06
- Context and the options weighed: the board estimates Stripe at about 3% plus payout costs (2026-09-02); refunds after a decline also incur fees. Options: Mentio's 20% covers the fees; the fees come out of the mentor's share.
- Decision and why: out of the mentor's share. The mentor receives their price minus Mentio's commission minus Stripe's fees.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: who bears the fees on a refunded session (decline or mentor no-show) is not decided. Revisit if invited mentors object.
- Status: active
