# D02 — Mentees find a mentor through the mentor's own link and through a public list mentors opt into
- Date, owner: 2026-10-27, Damian (founder C), confirmed in the om-discover quick pass
- Context and the options weighed: (a) the mentor's link only; (b) link plus a public list for mentors who opt in; (c) leave open. The staff engineer (2026-09-03) refused to be listed next to strangers; the senior Python engineer (2026-09-03) would join only alongside people they know; 23 of 109 Discord help threads (2026-08-18 to 31) got no reply, and a stranger with a blocker knows no mentor's link. No recommendation was given.
- Decision and why: (b). The link serves mentors who bring their own audience and those who refuse a list; the opt-in list serves mentees who know no mentor.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: being on the list must be the mentor's choice. Revisit if invited mentors decline because a list exists, or if the list stays empty.
